VPN Assessment Checklist
1. Check Authentication

Questions:

Is MFA enabled?
Are default accounts disabled?
Are passwords strong?
Are shared accounts used?
2. Check Encryption

Verify:

AES-256 encryption
SHA-256 or stronger hashing
Modern TLS versions
Strong key exchange

Avoid:

DES
3DES (legacy)
RC4
MD5
SHA-1 (where stronger options are available)
3. Verify VPN Protocol

Preferred:

IPsec
SSL VPN
OpenVPN
WireGuard

Avoid:

PPTP
4. Split Tunneling

Split tunneling allows Internet traffic to bypass the VPN while only company traffic goes through it.

Example:

Laptop
   │
 ├── Internet (Direct)
 └── Company (VPN)

Risk: An infected device can potentially act as a bridge between the Internet and the corporate network.

Many organizations disable split tunneling for higher security.

5. Idle Session Timeout

Ensure idle VPN sessions disconnect automatically after a defined period.

Example:

Disconnect after 15–30 minutes of inactivity.
6. VPN Logging

Check whether logs record:

Login attempts
Failed logins
Successful logins
Source IPs
Session duration
Administrative changes

Logs are important for monitoring and incident response.

7. Access Control

Users should only access the resources they need.

Example:

HR User
→ HR Server

IT Admin
→ Entire Network

This follows the principle of least privilege.

8. Patch Management

Verify:

VPN gateway firmware is current.
Security patches are applied.
No known vulnerabilities remain unaddressed.
