1. Policy = "WHAT is expected?"

A policy is a high-level rule made by management.

Example

"Everyone must wear a seatbelt."

It doesn't tell you how to wear it or when exactly—it simply states the rule.

GRC Example

Password Policy

Every employee must use a strong password with at least 12 characters.

Notice it doesn't explain how to create one.

Memory Trick

Policy = Promise

"This is our rule."
