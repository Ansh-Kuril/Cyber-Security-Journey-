ISO 27001:2022

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is an international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS).

It tells organizations how to protect their information using a structured management system.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISO
= International Organization for Standardization

IEC
= International Electrotechnical Commission

27001
= Standard number for Information Security Management Systems

2022
= Latest version released in 2022
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Annex A (Most Important Part)

ISO 27001:2022 contains 93 security controls, grouped into four themes.

Theme	                 Examples

Organizational	      Policies, roles, risk management
People	              Background verification, awareness, training
Physical	      CCTV, visitor logs, server room access
Technological	      Firewalls, MFA, encryption, backups
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISO 27001 has 10 clauses.

Clause	Name	                       Mandatory?

1	Scope	                       Informational
2	Normative References	       Informational
3	Terms & Definitions	       Informational
4	Context of the Organization	   yes
5	Leadership	                   yes
6	Planning	                   yes
7	Support	                           yes
8	Operation	                   yes
9	Performance Evaluation	           yes
10	Improvement	                   yes

Only Clauses 4–10 contain requirements for certification.

Where are we? → Clause 4
Who is leading? → Clause 5
What's our plan? → Clause 6
Do we have resources? → Clause 7
Start treating patients → Clause 8
Is everything working? → Clause 9
Improve mistakes → Clause 10
