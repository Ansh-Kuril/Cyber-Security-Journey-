GUIDLINES

> Recommendations on some concepts, technology or risk

.wording in guidelines matters
. avoid words like shall, Will or Must

Guideline = "Recommended Best Practice"

A guideline is not mandatory.

It's advice.

Car example:

Keep both hands on the steering wheel.

Good idea.

But sometimes you need one hand to shift gears.

GRC Example

It is recommended to use a password manager.

Nobody forces you.

It's simply considered best practice.

Another example:

Employees are encouraged to lock their computers whenever they leave their desk.

Recommended, but may not always be mandatory.

EXCEPTIONS 

> Avoid controls to business needs

>document Exceptions:

.Why can we not meet the requirements
.What are the associated risk to the exceptions
.plan of action and milestones (POA&Ms) to meet resurgent

Exception = "Permission to break the rule"

Sometimes a rule cannot be followed for a valid reason.

That's an exception.

Car example:

Normally:

Stop at every red light.

Exception:

Police officer waves you through.

You're allowed to break the normal rule.

GRC Example

Policy:

USB drives are prohibited.

Exception:

The Security Manager approves a USB for a forensic investigation.

Rule temporarily broken—with approval.
