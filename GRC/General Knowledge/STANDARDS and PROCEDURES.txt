Standard = "HOW GOOD should it be?"

A standard gives specific measurable requirements that everyone must follow.

Continuing the car example:

Policy:

Wear a seatbelt.

Standard:

The seatbelt must meet ISO safety standards and be locked before the car moves.

GRC Example

Policy:

Use strong passwords.

Standard:

Minimum 12 characters
One uppercase
One lowercase
One number
One special character

Now everyone knows exactly what "strong" means.

Example: Incident REsponse Procedure

Procedure = "HOW do you do it?"

A procedure is a step-by-step instruction.

Car example:

Policy:

Wear seatbelt.

Procedure:

Sit in driver's seat.
Pull belt.
Insert into buckle.
Check it clicks.
GRC Example

Password Reset Procedure

Open password portal.
Verify identity using OTP.
Create new password.
Confirm password.
Log in again.
