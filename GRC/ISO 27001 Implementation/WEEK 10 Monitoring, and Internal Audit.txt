WEEK 10: Monitoring, Review, and Internal Audit

DAY 39-41: Monitor and Measure ISMS Performance (Clause 9.1)

1. Objective:
   >Continuously monitor and measure the effectiveness of the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

2. Activities:

   >Establish Metrics: The organization establishes Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as the number of security incidents detected, average incident response time, and compliance with internal security policies.

   >Regular Monitoring: The consultant advises on tools and techniques to continuously monitor these metrics and evaluate the performance of the ISMS.

3. Documentation Created:

   >Monitoring and Measurement Records: Logs of security incidents, audit findings, KPI reports, monitoring results, and overall ISMS performance records.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 42-44: Conduct Internal Audits (Clause 9.2)

1. Objective:
   >Verify the ISMS's compliance and effectiveness.

2. Activities:

   >Audit Planning: The consultant develops an internal audit plan covering all ISMS-related processes, audit scope, criteria, schedule, and assigned auditors.

   >Conduct Audits: Internal audits are conducted to verify compliance with ISO 27001:2022 requirements, identify non-conformities, assess the effectiveness of implemented controls, and recommend corrective actions.

3. Documentation Created:

   >Internal Audit Reports: Summarize audit findings, non-conformities, observations, evidence collected, and recommendations for corrective actions.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAY 45-47: Management Review (Clause 9.3)

1. Objective:

   >Review the performance and effectiveness of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) at the management level.

2. Activities:

   >Prepare Review Documents: The consultant compiles internal audit reports, performance metrics, incident logs, risk assessment updates, and other relevant documents for the management review meeting.

   >Conduct the Review: Top management reviews the effectiveness of the ISMS, evaluates security objectives, identifies opportunities for improvement, reviews resource requirements, and decides on any necessary changes to policies, procedures, or controls.

3. Documentation Created:

   >Management Review Minutes: Records the meeting discussions, decisions made, action items, responsibilities assigned, and timelines for implementation.
