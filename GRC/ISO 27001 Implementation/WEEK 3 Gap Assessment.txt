WEEK 3: Gap Assessment and Risk Maangment Planning 

DAY 9-11: Conduct a Gap Assessment

1. Objective: identify gaps between the current practices and ISO 27001:2022 requirements. 
(first benchmark is to check clause 4 to 10 and Annexure A)

2. Activities: 

   >Gap Analysis: The consultant reviews existing policies such as data protection and access control against the ISO 27001 requirements it is discovered that while ABC consulting has some controls in place they lack format risk assessment and incident response process

   > Document Gaps: The consultant documents these gaps and provides recommendations for improvement 

3. Documentation Created: 

   >Gap Assessment Report: Highlights the gaps between current practices and ISO 27001 requirements, along with recommendations for closing these gaps.


DAY 12-14: Develop the Risk Management Process (Clause 6.1)

1. Objective: Establish a formal risk management framework

2. Activities 

   >Risk Assessment Methodology: The Consultant develops a methodology that includes identifying assets, assessing threats and vulnerabilities and evaluating risk 

  >Risk Criteria: The firm establishes criteria for risks evaluation, including scales for likelihood and impact

3. Documentation Created: 

  >risk management plan: Outlines the process for risk assessment including criteria for evaluation risk and plan for addressing them
