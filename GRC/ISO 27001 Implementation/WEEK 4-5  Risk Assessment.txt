WEEK 4-5:  Risk Assessment and Statement of Applicability 

DAY 15-18: Conduct Risk Assessment(Clause 6.1.2)

3 types of Risk assessments 

.Process: Training Services 
.Scenario: suppose phishing attack appears
.Asset: customer database 

1. Objective: Identify and assess risk to the organization information security 

2. Activates: 

   >Asset identification: The firm identifies critical assets such as customer database employee record and the CRM system 

   >Risk Evaluation: Each asset is evaluated for potential threats(e.g. unauthorized access, data breach) and vulnerabilities (e.g. weak passwords, outdated software). The consultant assess the likelihood and impact of each risk 

3 Documentation Created:

   >Risk assessment Reports: Details the risk identified, their likelihood, impact and overall risk levels 

DAY 19-21: Develop the statement of Applicability (Clause 6.1.3)

1. Objective: Determine which controls for Annex A will be implemented 

2. Activities: 

   >Control Selection: The consultant works with the project manager to select controls that address the identified risk for example multi factor authentication is chose to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access to the CRM system 

  .Document Applicability: The selected controls are documented with justifications for their inclusion. 

3. Documentations Created: 

   >Statement of Applicability: List the controls selected form Annex A and provides reasons for their selection or exclusion.
