WEEK 8-9: Implementation of Controls and Awareness

DAY 31-35: Operational Planning and Control (Clause 8.1)

1. Objective:

   >Implement the selected information security controls within the organization.

2. Activities:

   >Technical Controls: The IT department, guided by the consultant, implements technical controls such as firewalls, encryption for sensitive data, and regular software updates.

   >Administrative Controls: Procedures such as regular internal audits, access reviews, and other administrative controls are established to ensure the effectiveness of the ISMS.

3. Documentation Created:

   >Control Implementation Records: Documentation containing details of each implemented control, the responsible parties, implementation dates, and evidence of completion.

DAY 36-38: Conduct Awareness and Training Programs (Clause 7.2)

1. Objective:
   >Ensure all staff understand their role in maintaining information security.

2. Activities:

   >Develop Training Materials: The consultant develops training modules on topics such as phishing awareness, password security, and incident reporting.

   >Conduct Training: All employees, from IT staff to administrative personnel, participate in training sessions to familiarize themselves with the ISMS policies and procedures.

3. Documentation Created:

   >Training Records: Includes attendance logs, training materials, assessment results, and feedback forms.
