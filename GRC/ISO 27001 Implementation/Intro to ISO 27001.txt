1. ISO/IEC 27001 is the international standard that specifies the requirement for establishing implementing maintain and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS)

2. Clauses 4 to 10 are the section within the standard that outline specific requirements (these clauses are mandatory for no matter what business )

3. Annex A is a crucial part of ISO 270001 it contain 93 information security control organized in 4 groups 
(people, Technology, Organization, physical)

Organization Obtain Certification against this Standards

ISO/IEC 27001 gives 2 type of certification 

1. Individual (2 types in this)
   .Auditor : Audit those standards 
   .Implementation : Implement those standard

2. Organization

BASIC TERMS 

1. Document 
2. Specification (specific data)
3. Record (evidence)
