Scenario 1: You work in a consulting company (Very Common)

Suppose your company helps clients become ISO 27001:2022 certified.

Your job may include:

Meeting potential clients (vendors/companies)
Understanding their business
Explaining why ISO 27001:2022 is important
Convincing them that certification will benefit them
Performing a gap assessment
Helping implement security controls
Preparing them for the certification audit
Supporting them until they receive the certificate

In this case, you act as both a consultant and advisor.

Scenario 2: You work in an internal GRC team

If you work inside a bank, IT company, fintech, etc., you usually don't sell ISO certification.

Instead, you:

Assess third-party vendors
Check whether vendors already have ISO 27001 certification
Review their security documents
Send security questionnaires
Identify risks
Recommend improvements
Ensure vendors meet your company's security requirements

Here you're evaluating vendors rather than convincing them to get certified.
