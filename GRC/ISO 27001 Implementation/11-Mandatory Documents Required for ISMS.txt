Mandatory Documents Required for ISO 27001:2022 Certification
1. ISMS Scope Statement

Defines the boundaries and applicability of the Information Security Management System (ISMS), including the business units, locations, assets, processes, and technologies covered by the ISMS.

2. Information Security Policy

A high-level policy approved by top management that outlines the organization's commitment to protecting information assets, achieving security objectives, and continually improving the ISMS.

3. Risk Assessment Methodology

Describes the organization's approach for identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and prioritizing information security risks, including the criteria used to determine risk levels.

4. Risk Treatment Plan

Documents the selected risk treatment options, security controls to be implemented, responsible personnel, implementation timelines, and the status of each treatment action.

5. Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Lists all Annex A controls, specifies whether each control is applicable or not, provides justification for exclusions, and references where each implemented control is documented.

6. Information Security Objectives

Defines measurable information security objectives that support the organization's business goals, along with methods for monitoring, evaluating, and reviewing their achievement.

7. Internal Audit Program and Reports

Includes the internal audit schedule, audit plans, audit findings, non-conformities, observations, recommendations, and evidence demonstrating that internal audits have been performed.

8. Corrective Action Reports (CAR)

Documents identified non-conformities, root cause analysis, corrective actions implemented, responsible personnel, target completion dates, and verification that the corrective actions were effective.

9. Management Review Minutes

Records the outcomes of management review meetings, including decisions made, review of ISMS performance, audit results, resource requirements, risks, opportunities, and action items.

10. Procedure Documents for Key Controls

Detailed procedures describing how critical information security controls are implemented and maintained, such as:

Access Control Procedure
Incident Management Procedure
Asset Management Procedure
Risk Assessment Procedure
Backup and Recovery Procedure
Change Management Procedure
Business Continuity Procedure
Supplier Security Procedure
Password Management Procedure
Data Classification and Handling Procedure
Media Handling Procedure
User Access Management Procedure
Vulnerability Management Procedure

These procedures provide evidence that the organization's security controls are consistently implemented and aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
