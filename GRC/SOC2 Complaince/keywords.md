* IAM: You log in with your company account.
* RBAC: As a Finance employee, you can only access finance systems.
* NDA: You promise not to share company secrets.
* UPS: Power goes out, but the servers stay on.
* DLP: You accidentally try to email customer data outside the company, and it's blocked.
* Escalation Matrix: A website outage is reported to the Help Desk, then the System Admin, then the IT Manager.
* CAB Approval: The team gets approval before updating the website.
* Rollback Plan: The update fails, so the previous version is restored.
* Backup Plan: Customer orders are backed up every night.
* Restore Testing: The team regularly confirms those backups can be restored.
* DR Plan: A ransomware attack encrypts the servers, so systems are restored from clean backups.
* BCP: Employees work remotely while IT restores the environment, and customers can still place orders.
