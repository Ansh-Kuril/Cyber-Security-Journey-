Trust Services Criteria (TSC)

SOC 2 revolves around five Trust Services Criteria.

Think of them as the five pillars.
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Security (Mandatory)

Protect systems against unauthorized access.

Examples

MFA
Firewalls
Antivirus
IAM
Encryption
SIEM
Logging

Without Security,

No SOC 2.
--------------------------------------------------------------
2. Availability

Is the system available when customers need it?

Examples

Backups
Disaster Recovery
Redundant servers
UPS
Monitoring

Example

Google Drive should not be offline for three days.
------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Processing Integrity

Does the system process data correctly?

Example

Online banking.

You transfer $100.

System should transfer exactly $100.

Not $10.

Not $1,000.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Confidentiality

Sensitive information stays confidential.

Examples

Encryption
Access Control
NDA
Secure Disposal

Only authorized users can access data.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Privacy

Personal information is collected and handled properly.

Examples

Consent
Data retention
Cookie policy
Right to delete data
Privacy notices

Mostly about Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
