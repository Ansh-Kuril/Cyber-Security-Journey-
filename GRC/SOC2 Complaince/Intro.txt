SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) is an audit framework developed by the AICPA (American Institute of Certified Public Accountants).

It helps organizations prove that they protect customer data securely.

Simply put:

SOC 2 is a report that proves a company has proper security controls to protect customer information.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why was SOC 2 created?

Imagine you're buying cloud storage.

Would you upload your company data to a random website?

Probably not.

You'd ask:

Is my data secure?
Can hackers steal it?
Do they take backups?
Do employees misuse my data?

SOC 2 answers those questions.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real Example

Imagine:

Company A develops payroll software.

Their customer asks:

"How do we know your system is secure?"

Instead of saying

"Trust us."

They provide a

SOC 2 Type II Report

Now the customer knows

Controls exist
Auditor verified them
Security processes work
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What is audited?

Not software.

Not source code.

Not applications.

The auditor checks

The company's controls.
